Fast Vocuabulary Tree Training Filip Ilic Not published
Abstract We present a parametrized vocabulary tree training algorithm. Its goal is to offer a speedup with respect to training time compared to a standard hierarchical descriptor clustering. We measure the performance of various settings of the proposed algorithm on multiple databases, compare it to a standard vocabulary tree building algorithm, offer intuition about parametrization depending on the database, and show that it is possible to decrease training time, while preserving recognition performance. Keywords

Vocabulary Tree, Bag of words, Image classification, TF-IDF
