Abstract<br>
<span class="post-meta">
    We present a parametrized vocabulary tree training algorithm. Its goal is to offer a speedup with respect to training time compared to a standard hierarchical descriptor clustering.
    We measure the performance of various settings of the proposed algorithm on multiple databases, compare it to a standard vocabulary tree building algorithm, offer intuition about parametrization depending on the database, and show that it is possible to decrease training time, while preserving recognition performance. 
</span>

Keywords<br>
