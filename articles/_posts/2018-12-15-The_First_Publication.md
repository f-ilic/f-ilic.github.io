During my Master's thesis I worked on a publication attempt at CVPR, which 
culmulated in luke warm review (weak accept, borderline, weak reject) and a final
rejection of the paper submission.
The reviews were written in good faith and suggested good ways to improve the paper.
Between the submission deadline and the final reviews I was still working on the project
because the Thesis was not complete yet, so I found many of the critisims of the reviews
myself in the 2 months between those two dates.

That is why I thought that I could convice them with with a strong rebuttal to the
problems that were pointed out, and mostly anticipated, and get our paper accepted.

It did not turn out this way.

Instead we submitted it out of proceedings at CVWW a smaller regional conference.
