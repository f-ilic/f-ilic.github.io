The <a href="https://www.greentech.at/jam2019/">greentech jam</a> is a "Interdisciplinary student hackathon on the topic of
'Code & Concept for Green Tech' ". In the course of 48 hours small teams of four students are asked to come up with solutions to real world problems faced by the industry. In essence various companies present small tasks which could be solved by either building a small prototype or developing a concept to solve the issue at hand.

## The Challenge
---

Our team, consisting of two computer scientists, an architect and a physicist chose the problem presented by Energie Graz.

Their problem is rather straight forward;
Houses that have a photovoltaic system need to spend the generated energy as soon as it is generated to avoid energy storage costs, or even taxes if the eletricity were to be fed into the power grid. The time at which the photovoltaic electricity is available is - unexpectedly - around noon; this is also the time at which people are generally not at home.

Our proposed solution was to provide a web interface in which the users could monitor the peak hours of the photovoltaic energy production, make informed decisions, and connect and control their smart devices to deal with the peak electricity output (i.e. start the dishwasher, charge the roomba, etc.)

## The Atmosphere
---
The whole event was well organized with provided snacks, drinks and enough creative space. Socially it was also a very nice experience, with likeminded people attending that wanted to get from an initial idea to a more refined concept within two days.

It was also very entertaining during the concept phase to see the widely different ideas each team member brought to the table.
Overall it was a very entertaining and refreshing experience, even though our team didn't win the competition.
